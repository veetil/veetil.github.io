## Axinet AI: company discovery and evidence-based qualification

[Product website](https://axinet.ai/)

I am the founder and technical builder of Axinet, which helps GTM teams discover and qualify target accounts from public web information. I built a system that interprets natural-language requirements and retrieves candidates through company-, document- and passage-level representations, keyword indexes and domain facets. It combines hybrid retrieval, reranking and staged qualification, using inexpensive models and decision systems for repeated checks and concentrating frontier-model review on selected cases.

My work spans extraction, entity resolution, retrieval and evidence evaluation. A central challenge is preserving distinctions such as supplier versus operator, planned versus completed activity, operational prerequisites and insufficient evidence. I have also developed experiments around deciding which cases need further research and which evidence warrants more expensive review.

## TinyTransformer: language-model architecture and training experiments

[Source code](https://github.com/veetil/TinyTransformer)

I implemented and trained GPT-2-style models in PyTorch, comparing a SwiGLU/RMSNorm variant with a baseline. The implementation includes configurable rotary embeddings and mixture-of-experts components, alongside distributed training, mixed precision, validation and checkpointing. This project gave me hands-on experience implementing architectural components and organizing controlled training comparisons.

## AI Scientist for Applied ML: experiment execution and evaluation

I built infrastructure coordinating dataset construction, coding, remote training and evaluation through specialist agents and persistent experiment artifacts. Completed experiments include sentiment baselines and a small Qwen3-4B QLoRA training and evaluation run on an A10G GPU. The system addresses the engineering needed to turn a research plan into an executable, inspectable experiment with recorded results.

## Research automation and contextual paper ranking

[Research automation project](/projects/0_project/) · [Paper evaluation project](/projects/1_project/)

I built research workflows for exploring ideas, inspecting related work and organizing implementation and evaluation. A related system uses adaptive pairwise comparisons and Elo-style ranking to prioritize papers against a concrete research objective.

My contribution spans problem formulation, system design and implementation. These projects address the problem of directing limited research time and model context toward information useful to a particular investigation.

## Agent-Evolve: iterative code optimization

[Project write-up](/projects/2_project/)

I developed an experimental system for iterative code improvement combining candidate generation, execution-based evaluation, population search and progressively more expensive checks. The design emphasizes rejecting invalid candidates early, preserving useful alternatives and maintaining an inspectable history of experiments.

## Research-paper review and explanation

I developed a paper-review system that brings together text, figures, code and related work to produce detailed explanations, workflow analyses and critical reviews. I also created a website for reviewing papers through concrete examples. This work addresses the practical problem of making technical literature useful for human readers and subsequent research workflows.

## Financial research and news-based stock-selection experiments

I developed financial hypotheses and models using research papers, books, spread data and news. This included volatility modeling and a news-based stock-selection system with validation experiments. The work highlighted the importance of explicit domain datasets, company relationships and operating context when interpreting information for a financial decision.
